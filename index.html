✋ PRIVACY POLICY • LAST UPDATED OCTOBER 2026

Your intimate wellness data is yours alone. tAlly⁺ is built on a local-first philosophy with zero advertising, zero cross-app tracking, and no monetization of personal information.

1. Local-First Storage by Default

By default, all encounters, partner directories, STI screening records, test results, notes, and medication schedules are stored on your physical iPhone.

We do not run background analytics, telemetry, or tracking servers.

2. Zero Advertising & No Data Selling

tAlly⁺ contains zero third-party advertising SDKs and zero cross-app trackers.

We never sell, broker, lease, or monetize your health, sexual activity, or identity data to any data broker, insurer, advertiser, or commercial entity.

3. Optional Cloud Backup (Google Firebase)

Cloud Backup is 100% optional. Only if you choose to sign in with Apple, Google, or Email, your records are uploaded to Google Firebase. Third-party sign-in providers (Apple and Google) and Google Firebase process data only to provide sign-in and backup.

What is uploaded: your encounters, partners, test results (including attached lab-result images), medications, reminders, notes, and profile (including avatar photo), plus your account email, unique user ID, and sync timestamps.

Backups are protected by HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and Google's encryption at rest. They are not end-to-end encrypted.

By signing in to Cloud Backup, you give your explicit consent to this upload of your health-related information. You can stop at any time by signing out or deleting your cloud account. Cloud data is deleted when you delete your account.

Product Updates (Optional): If you opt in to product updates during account creation or in Account Settings, we may send occasional feature announcements or tips to your registered email. You can opt out at any time directly in Account Settings or via the unsubscribe link in any message.

4. Device Hardware Permissions

Photo Library & Camera: Used only when you choose to attach an avatar or a lab-result image. Photos are stored on your device. If you enable Cloud Backup, they are included in your backup.

Face ID & Touch ID: Used only for local on-device app unlock. Biometric data never leaves Apple's Secure Enclave.

Local Notifications: Scheduled on your device for medication and screening reminders. If explicitly enabled in Settings, occasional product announcements may also be delivered. You can disable announcement alerts anytime in Notification Settings.

5. Your Rights & Data Control

Export: generate a JSON export of your records anytime in Settings. It is not encrypted, so store it somewhere safe.

Archived reminders: deleted reminders moved to Archived are retained locally for 30 days so you can restore them if needed, after which they are automatically and permanently deleted (or you can permanently delete them immediately at any time).

Erase local data: 'Erase All Local Data' in Settings permanently wipes all local records, passcodes, and security questions from your device.

Delete cloud account: permanently delete your cloud account and all synced backups in Settings > Account & Cloud Backup > Delete Cloud Account.

tAlly⁺ is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to minors, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.
