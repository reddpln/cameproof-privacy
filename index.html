Transparency & Data Rights

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

🔒 Local-First, Privacy-First Architecture: CameProof does NOT operate remote media servers. Your captured photos, recorded videos, and GPS coordinates remain strictly on your iPhone and in your local Apple Photos library.

1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

CameProof ("we", "us", or "our") respects your personal and business privacy. We believe that visual proof recorded for compliance, deliveries, incidents, or workplace inspections is sensitive, proprietary, and strictly belongs to you.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Media Uploads: Photos and videos captured using CameProof are processed and stamped locally using CoreGraphics on your device. We never transmit, inspect, or store your media on cloud servers.
  • No Personal Profile Tracking: CameProof does not require account creation, passwords, or personal identity profiles to use the camera.
  • No Watermark Exfiltration: Stamped notes, company names, and custom logos reside entirely on your device.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Required exclusively to stream real-time viewfinders and capture high-resolution photo and video evidence.
  • Location (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription): Used strictly while the camera is active to calculate verified latitude, longitude, and reverse-geocode physical street addresses onto the visual watermark ribbon. Location is never tracked in the background.
  • Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription): Required to record audio during video proof capture. Audio is processed on-device and never sent to external servers.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription & NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Required to automatically save your captured proof photos and videos to your Camera Roll and display them in the in-app album.

4. Third-Party Services & Subscriptions

  • In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2): Subscription transactions (CameProof Pro Weekly, Monthly, Yearly) are processed directly by Apple. We use Apple's private iCloud Key-Value Store (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) to securely recognize your active subscription across your personal devices under the same Apple ID.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): For non-paying (Free Tier) users, CameProof displays non-intrusive sponsor ads via Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may use advertising identifiers subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) GDPR/UK privacy consent. Subscribers of CameProof Pro receive a 100% ad-free experience.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because media is stored locally in your camera roll and app sandbox, you retain complete control over your content. Deleting a photo in CameProof or Apple Photos permanently erases it from your device.
